💼 Featured Projects

🏫 Campus Connect

A centralized web platform helping students reconnect with their lost belongings on campus.
  • The Challenge: Campuses often lack a reliable, streamlined way for students to report lost items or return found property, leading to cluttered physical lost-and-found desks and lost valuables.
  • The Solution: Designed an intuitive, real-time matching dashboard where users can quickly upload photos and descriptions of found items, filter by location, and securely message owners—built with a highly responsive frontend for mobile-first students.

👟 ShoeEase

An elegant e-commerce experience built on the philosophy that great footwear is the foundation of confidence and comfort.
  • The Challenge: Many online shoe retailers have cluttered interfaces that complicate the filtering, sizing, and checkout processes, creating friction for buyers looking for affordable, quality shoes.
  • The Solution: Designed a clean, minimal user experience with smooth product transitions, interactive size-picking elements, and a lightning-fast checkout flow to deliver on the brand's mission of stylish, durable, and accessible footwear.
